## Red Widow
Formed on occasion from human blood splashed on sunless soil and allowed to clog for years, red widows are spiders, around the size of a kitten, that feed on fear and anger, which their venom can induce uncontrollably for a few minutes. If allowed, they will hide in households and bite the residents in their sleep, producing terrible nightmares and -on rare occasion- people to awake and murder their lovers. Their feeding causes exhaustion and, on the long term, dementia and death. They're most often found on prisons and warzone s. While they're harmful, they're considered by many to be a boon- they can feed on latent emotion and spirits, and are believed to prevent demons from fully forming and undergoing the lazarus process.

## ranchas

It's unknown if these are a natural but extremely furtive species, a form of spirit, or the result of mice consuming human corpses whose spirit is gestating into a demon. Ranchas are rats possessing intelligence equivalent to a young child, poisonous bile in the place of blood, and on occasion magic powers, who will sometimes be found in places where many humans have died. While they can communicate with humans in a rudimentary manner (they don't often know more than one or two words, but can gesticulate and understand clear enough drawings) they prefer not to and forget quickly if they did. They steal shinny objects, or anything that makes an interesting enough sound, and have been known to execute complex plans to steal these safely. More direly, they can fashion or use simple weapons, and quickly acquire a taste for human meat after growing used to their presence. In one case, a group of these learned to use firearms, which led to a mass shooting.
